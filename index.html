Day-by-day plan

Your island-to-highlands journey

Day 1

Edinburgh arrival

Old Town / Stockbridge

  • Check into a central guesthouse or apartment.
  • Walk the Royal Mile and watch sunset from Calton Hill.
  • Keep dinner easy and local with a relaxed final evening.
Budget $260–$340
Day 2

Edinburgh + coast

North Berwick / Dunbar

  • Visit Edinburgh Castle or the National Museum.
  • Drive to the coast for sea air and beach views.
  • Optional surf lesson if the swell is right.
Budget $290–$440
Day 3

Loch Lomond

Luss / Balloch

  • Take a scenic drive through the Trossachs.
  • Enjoy a lakeside walk or short boat ride.
  • Stay in a cozy village guesthouse by the water.
Budget $260–$350
Day 4

Glen Coe to Fort William

Highlands

  • Drive through Glen Coe and stop at panoramic viewpoints.
  • Keep the afternoon light with a short hike or lakeside stroll.
  • Warm up with a hearty pub dinner in the evening.
Budget $260–$370
Day 5

Surf day

West coast beach

  • Choose a beginner-friendly surf spot based on swell forecast.
  • Morning lesson, casual lunch, then a slow afternoon.
  • Finish with seafood and a quiet night by the coast.
Budget $350–$480
Day 6

Scenic west coast

Skye or Arisaig

  • Choose one big highlight: castle, boat trip, or dramatic viewpoint.
  • Keep other stops flexible for a slower pace.
  • Stay somewhere with a view and a relaxed evening feel.
Budget $340–$510
Day 7

Return to Edinburgh

Stirling / Firth of Forth

  • Drive east with a stop at Stirling or a scenic viewpoint.
  • Enjoy one final relaxed meal before departure.
  • Leave room for last-minute rest and recovery.
Budget $260–$330

Travel tips

Make the trip smoother

Book smart

Choose B&Bs or guesthouses in smaller towns to keep costs down without sacrificing comfort.

Check the surf

West coast conditions change quickly. Keep the surf day flexible and let the forecast guide your plan.

Pace the roads

Scotland rewards slower driving—leave room for scenic stops and photogenic detours.

Keep one paid stop daily

Mix one major activity with free beaches, hikes, and viewpoints to stay within budget.
